## Summary
JobLoss OS is an unofficial organizer for people applying for unemployment benefits in Texas. It is **not** the Texas
Workforce Commission or any government agency. The app works without an account, and **what you enter stays on your device**.

## What the app stores (on your device only)
Dates you enter (e.g. last day worked, application date), letters you log (type, dates, notes, photos), things you record as
submitted (type, date, confirmation numbers, notes, screenshots), your work search log, status notes, questions, and settings.

## What the app does not collect
We do not ask for, and ask you not to enter, your Social Security number, passwords, PINs, bank or card details. The app has
no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no account. We (the publisher) do not receive any of your data.

## Permissions
Notifications (reminders you turn on), camera and photos (only when you choose to attach an image).

## Sharing

recipient's handling is outside our control.
