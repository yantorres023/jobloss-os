What do I do today?

First steps after a layoff, WorkInTexas registration, your payment-request day every two weeks.

What deadline is next?

Log a letter and enter the date printed on it. The date on your letter always wins over the app's estimate.

What did I already submit?

Confirmation numbers, screenshots and a work search log with the same fields as TWC's form.

When do I need a person?

Denials, appeals, hearings and wage problems point you to TWC's phone lines and free legal aid.

What it isn't

  • It isn't TWC. You apply and request payments on TWC's own website or phone line.
  • It can't tell you whether you qualify or whether to appeal. Only TWC decides claims; legal aid can advise you.
  • It never asks for your Social Security number, passwords, PIN or bank details. If something does, it isn't us.
