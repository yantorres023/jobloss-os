## Full description
JobLoss OS is an independent, unofficial app. It is not the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) and is not connected to any
government agency. It does not give legal advice and can't file anything for you.

Lost your job in Texas? JobLoss OS helps you:
• See what to do today — each step links to the official TWC or federal page it comes from.
• Know your next deadline — log a letter and enter the date printed on it. The date on your letter always wins.
• Remember your filing day every two weeks, and record your weekly work search with the same fields as TWC's log.
• Keep proof — save confirmation numbers and screenshots of what you submitted.
• Prepare questions for TWC and find free legal aid when you need a person.

Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking. What you enter stays on your phone. The app never asks for your Social
Security number, passwords, PIN or bank details.
