# JOBS TO BE DONE — JobLoss OS

Derived from official process (OR/OG) and weak community signals (CE). No interviews. All are **hypotheses** until beta.

## Core job
> When I've just been laid off in Texas and money is tight, help me **do the right official step today** and **keep proof of
> what I did**, so I don't lose benefits through a missed step or a lost record.

## Job map

| Stage | Functional job | Emotional job | Official anchor | Product response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0–3 | Know what to do first; apply; register on WorkInTexas | "Don't let me screw this up while panicking" | A1, A4, A19 | First-72-hours checklist, one card at a time |
| Week 1–2 | Understand letters (wages statement, payment instructions, ID verification, work search letter) | "What is this letter and does it need action?" | A6, A9, A12, A13 | "Log a letter" → type → date printed on it → task + reminder |
| Every 2 weeks | Request payment on my filing day; report earnings honestly | "Don't miss the day" | A5, A8 | Filing-day reminders; submission log with confirmation # + screenshot |
| Every week | Do N work search activities and record them | "If they ask, can I prove it?" | A9–A11 | Work search log with TWC form fields; weekly count vs number **from my letter** |
| Any time | Respond when TWC contacts me; RESEA orientation | "Did I miss something?" | A18, A19 | Letter-driven tasks with dates |
| If denied | Know the appeal deadline; get help | "Is it too late? Who can help?" | A14–A17, S24–S25 | Deadline from notice; "get help" routing; question list. **No advice on whether to appeal.** |
| Health | Keep health coverage | "Will my kids be covered?" | B1, B2 | 60-day Marketplace window tracker; COBRA notice deadline |
| Later | Show everything to a lawyer/agency rep | "I need my records in one place" | — | Export (text summary + JSON + attachments list) |

## Anti-jobs (explicitly not served)
- "Tell me if I'm eligible." → Only TWC decides. App says so.
- "Should I appeal?" → App shows the deadline and who can advise (legal aid, TWC).
- "File for me / log in for me." → Never. No credentials, no automation.
