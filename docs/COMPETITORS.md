## Positioning

"**Free, private, Texas-only layoff organizer.** Your letters, your deadlines, your proof — on your phone, with the official
link for every step. Not TWC. Not legal advice."

Differentiators we can defend: (1) notice-driven deadlines with "the date printed on your letter wins", (2) proof vault
(screenshots/confirmation numbers per submission), (3) local-first/no account, (4) official-vs-suggested labeling per step,
(5) "when to get help" routing to legal aid/TWC.

Undefended: work-search logging alone (WorkSearchLog already does it), certification itself (only TWC can).
